## Notes on Resources

### HuggingFace NLP Course
- Focus on Chapters 1-3 for fundamentals
- Chapter 6 covers tokenizers in depth
- Hands-on exercises are valuable

### Jay Alammar Blogs
- Read Word2Vec first, then BERT
- Builds intuition for why embeddings work
- Great visualizations to reference later
