# Resources: How LLMs Work

## Primary Sources (Start Here)

| Resource | Type | Duration | Why It's Essential |
|----------|------|----------|-------------------|
| [Andrej Karpathy — Let's build GPT from scratch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kCc8FmEb1nY) | Video | ~2h | The gold standard. Builds GPT from zero, explains every component. |
| [3Blue1Brown — But what is a GPT?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wjZofJX0v4M) | Video | ~1h | Beautiful visualizations of attention and transformers. |
| [3Blue1Brown — Attention in Transformers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eMlx5fFNoYc) | Video | ~30m | Deep dive into attention mechanism with visual intuition. |

## Notes on Resources

### Karpathy Video
- Watch at 1x speed first time, he moves fast
- Code along if possible, builds intuition
- Focus on the "why" not just the "what"

### 3Blue1Brown
- Watch after Karpathy for visual reinforcement
- Great for building intuition about matrix operations
