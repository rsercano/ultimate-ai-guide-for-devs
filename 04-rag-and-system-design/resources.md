## Notes on Resources

### Full Stack Deep Learning
- Focus on the system architecture lectures
- Practical, not theoretical
- Covers deployment, monitoring, iteration

### Chip Huyen
- Industry perspective (Snorkel, Claypot AI)
- Balance of theory and practice
- Her book is broader than just LLMs but very relevant
