## Who This Is For

- **Software developers** (any language/stack) wanting to understand AI deeply
- **Engineers transitioning** into AI/ML engineering roles
- **Technical leads** who need to architect AI systems
- **Anyone** tired of using LLMs as a black box

### Prerequisites

- Programming experience (Python preferred)
- Curiosity and patience
- No ML/AI background required

## How to Use This Guide

### 1. Follow the Order
Each module builds on previous concepts. Don't skip ahead.

### 2. Primary Sources First
Watch/read the linked resources, then read the notes for reinforcement.

### 3. Complete the Checkpoints
Each module has a checkpoint—verify your understanding before moving on.

### 4. Build Projects
Theory without practice doesn't stick. Module 07 has graduated projects.

### 5. Take Your Time
This isn't a weekend tutorial. Deep understanding takes weeks, not hours.
