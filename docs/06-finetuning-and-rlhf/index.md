
# Module 06: Fine-tuning & RLHF

## Objective

Answer the questions: **"When and how should I customize a model? How do models like ChatGPT become helpful?"**

After this module, you should understand the full training pipeline from pretraining to alignment, and when fine-tuning makes sense for your use case.

## Prerequisites

- [Modules 01-05](../01-how-llms-work/) completed
- Understanding of basic training concepts (Module 02)

| 03 | [RLHF and Alignment](./03-rlhf-and-alignment.md) | How models become helpful |

## Checkpoint

After completing this module, you should be able to:

- [ ] Explain the three-stage LLM training pipeline
- [ ] Choose between full fine-tuning and parameter-efficient methods
- [ ] Describe what RLHF optimizes for
- [ ] Identify when fine-tuning is and isn't appropriate
- [ ] Understand alignment techniques (RLHF, DPO, Constitutional AI)
