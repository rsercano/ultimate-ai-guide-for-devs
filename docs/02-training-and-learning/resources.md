## Notes on Resources

### 3Blue1Brown Neural Networks Series
- Watch all 4 videos in order
- Focus on building intuition, not memorizing formulas
- The backprop video is especially important

### StatQuest
- Josh's style is very accessible
- Good for "I know this works but why?" questions
- Watch multiple videos for connected concepts
